AUTOMATION DIVISION

ROBO SOCCER BOT

An omnidirectional, high-speed sports robot equipped with custom solenoid/pneumatic kicking pistons and active ball-dribbler rollers.

STEP 01

OMNIDIRECTIONAL DRIVE SYSTEM

Utilizes 4 triple-layer omni wheels mounted at 90-degree offsets, enabling instant lateral strafing without needing to rotate the chassis.

STEP 02

PNEUMATIC KICKING MECHANISM

A compressed solenoid piston triggers 8m/s linear strike kicks to drive soccer balls across arena targets with pin-point accuracy.
