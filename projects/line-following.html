SPEED TRACK DIVISION

HIGH-SPEED LINE-FOLLOWING BOT

A precision speedster leveraging differential motor feedback and 8-channel QTR sensors to trace complex tight-radius tracks.

STEP 01

INFRARED SENSOR ARRAY BAR

Front-mounted 8-channel reflectance array detects line offsets at 2.5kHz sampling rate for smooth differential correction.

STEP 02

DYNAMIC PID LOOP TUNING

Proportional-Integral-Derivative algorithm eliminates wobble on straightaways while aggressively banking into tight 90-degree corners.
