ABOUT KREATORZ

We are more than a club. We are a hardware launchpad turning raw engineering passion into battle-tested reality at MVGR College of Engineering.

OUR ORIGIN

Born in the Labs

Kreatorz was forged out of a quiet realization: engineering isn't learned solely from textbooks. In 2021, a small group of MVGR students reclaimed a corner of the mechanics lab, set up an old soldering station, and began building.

Today, that same corner is a fully equipped lab pushing boundaries in firmware, mechanical structure, and robotic navigation.

CHRONOLOGY OF INNOVATION

OUR MILESTONES

2023

Club Inception

Founded by 5 hardware enthusiasts in the MVGR mechanical lab with a single 3D printer.

2024

First Hackathon Triumph

Captured 1st Place at National Rover Challenge with our custom suspension rover chassis.

2025

National Recognition

Design patent granted for agricultural drone spraying system; roster expanded to 50+ builders.

2026

Global Horizons

Venturing into autonomous indoor navigation (ROS) and custom planetary rovers.
